# Cold Emailing Skill

**Signal-Based Outreach Strategy**

> "They don't feel sold. They feel seen."
> — Reese Queen (905 opportunities in 28 days)

---

## Core Philosophy

Traditional cold email: **Filters → Blast → Hope**
Signal-based cold email: **Signals → Context → Connection**

The difference: Instead of filtering by job title/company size and sending generic pitches, find where prospects are **actively engaging right now** and build outreach around **what they just said or did**.

---

## The 4-Step Framework

### 1. Find Engagement Signals

Where are your best prospects **actively talking** right now?

| Signal Source | What to Look For |
|---------------|------------------|
| **Podcasts** | Guests on relevant shows, topics they discussed |
| **LinkedIn** | Posts, comments, articles they wrote/engaged with |
| **Communities** | Slack groups, Discord, Reddit, forums they're active in |
| **Events** | Conferences they attended/spoke at, webinars |
| **Twitter/X** | Threads, replies, topics they're vocal about |
| **Content** | Blog posts, YouTube videos, newsletters they publish |

**Key insight:** Recent activity > Static firmographic data

### 2. Pull the Context

For each prospect, capture:
- **What they care about** (topics, pain points, interests)
- **What they're saying** (actual quotes, opinions, takes)
- **What they're engaged in right now** (recent activity, not 2-year-old blog post)

**Example context pull:**
```
Prospect: Sarah Chen, VP Engineering at ScaleUp Inc
Signal: Guest on "Engineering Leadership" podcast (Mar 15)
Context: Talked about challenges scaling from 20→100 engineers, 
         specifically mentioned "hiring senior folks who can mentor"
```

### 3. Build Outreach Around Context

The email should **reference the signal** so they know you're paying attention.

**❌ Generic (feels like spam):**
```
Hi Sarah,
I noticed you're VP Engineering at ScaleUp. We help companies 
scale their engineering teams...
```

**✅ Signal-based (feels seen):**
```
Hi Sarah,
Caught your episode on Engineering Leadership — your point about 
senior hires who can actually mentor (not just code) really resonated.

We've helped 3 similar-stage companies build that exact bench. 
One thing that worked: [specific insight]...
```

### 4. Automate at Scale

Don't manually research one prospect at a time. Build systems:

1. **Signal scraping** — Monitor podcasts, LinkedIn, communities
2. **Context extraction** — Pull relevant quotes/topics automatically
3. **Template matching** — Route signals to relevant email templates
4. **Campaign automation** — Launch campaigns around the clock

**Tools to explore:**
- Instantly, Lemlist, Apollo (sending)
- Phantombuster, Clay (data enrichment)
- Podcast guest databases
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Custom scrapers for communities

## Email Templates by Signal Type

### Podcast Guest

```
Subject: Your [Podcast Name] episode

Hey {FirstName},

Just listened to your chat with [Host] on [Podcast] — 
{specific_point_they_made} really stuck with me.

{Bridge to your offer based on what they discussed}

{Soft CTA}

{Signature}
```

### LinkedIn Post/Comment

```
Subject: Re: your post on {topic}

{FirstName} — saw your take on {topic}. 

{Agree/add nuance/share related insight}

{Natural bridge to conversation}
```

### Event Speaker/Attendee

```
Subject: After {Event Name}

Hey {FirstName},

{Reference their talk/panel OR the event theme they attended}

{Relevant insight or offer}
```

- [ ] Track and iterate on what signals convert best
